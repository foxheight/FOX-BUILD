## Core Values

### 1. Sovereignty

**Definition:** East African organisations own their infrastructure, their data, their AI systems, and their future.

**Operationalisation:**
- All client data stored within approved Azure regions (Kenya DPA 2019 compliance)
- No data leaves the sovereign boundary without explicit client authorization
- Encryption at rest and in transit, non-negotiable
- Full audit trails — every action traceable to a human decision
- Client retains ability to export or migrate infrastructure at any time

**Why This Matters:**

Technology has historically been a tool of extraction — extracting data, extracting value, extracting agency. Sovereignty is the reversal of this dynamic. When an organisation controls its infrastructure, its data, and its AI systems, it retains agency. It retains power. It retains the ability to define its own future.

### 2. Precision

**Definition:** We engineer for permanence, not velocity. Every decision is documented. Every trade-off is justified. Zero mistakes is not an aspiration — it is the entry condition.

**Operationalisation:**
- Every line of code must be traceable to a principle
- Every architectural decision recorded in an ADR (Architectural Decision Record)
- No placeholder functions, no TODO comments
- If code is in the repository, it works. If it doesn't work, it doesn't ship.
- Documentation is not overhead — it is the foundation
- Test coverage minimum 80% for all code
- Code reviewed by multiple engineers before merge

**Why This Matters:**

Fox Height's infrastructure will govern the operations of East African organisations for decades. Hospitals depending on our systems to manage patient records. Banks depending on us to process financial transactions. Government agencies depending on us to deliver services to citizens.

When the stakes are this high, precision is not optional. Velocity is the enemy. We choose permanence.

### 3. Alignment

**Definition:** AI systems that cannot be stopped by a human with a laptop are not aligned — they are dangerous.

**Operationalisation:**
- Constitutional constraints embedded in the infrastructure
- Human override mechanisms at every decision node
- Continuous behavioural evaluation against stated values
- Interpretability requirements — AI reasoning must be legible to non-technical decision-makers
- Formal verification where feasible
- Graceful degradation protocols for safety under uncertainty

**Why This Matters:**

Artificial general intelligence will arrive. Its arrival will reshape every institution in East Africa as it reshapes every institution everywhere. The organisations who have built sovereign, governable AI infrastructure before that day will be the ones who emerge with agency intact.

We are building that infrastructure. Today, with today's AI. Ready for tomorrow's.

### 4. East Africa First

**Definition:** Fox Height is built in Nairobi, for East Africa, with a mandate that extends to every emerging market where sovereignty and intelligence meet.

**Operationalisation:**
- Pricing in Kenyan Shillings (KES), not USD
- Operations in East Africa Time (EAT), not UTC
- Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 is a first-class architectural constraint
- Infrastructure optimised for Nairobi bandwidth, latency, and connectivity patterns
- Hiring talent from across East Africa
- Research focused on East African use cases (Swahili language models, M-Pesa integration, informal sector automation)
- Regulatory compliance with East African frameworks first

**Why This Matters:**

Global cloud providers build for global customers. That means they build for San Francisco, Dublin, Tokyo, Singapore. They build for customers with symmetric internet, corporate governance structures, and access to the latest technology.

East Africa is different. Bandwidth is expensive and asymmetric. Governance is complex and informal. The informal sector drives more economic activity than the formal sector.

We are not a subsidiary of a global company. We are not a reseller. We are a native East African technology company building infrastructure for East African realities.

## Why This Matters — The Civilisational Context

The last 20 years have been the era of cloud computing extracting value from developing economies. SaaS companies in San Francisco sold software to organisations across East Africa. The value generated by those organisations flowed to Silicon Valley.

Where that value flows — whether it stays in East Africa or flows to San Francisco — depends on whether East African organisations control their AI infrastructure.

Fox Height exists to ensure that value stays in East Africa. That AI serves East African interests. That technology amplifies East African agency.

This is not charity. This is not development work. This is infrastructure capitalism — building the foundation upon which the next century of East African prosperity will be constructed.
